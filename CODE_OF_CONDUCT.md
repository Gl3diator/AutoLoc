# AutoLoc — Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in AutoLoc
a welcoming, respectful and harassment-free experience
for contributors of all backgrounds and experience levels.

## Our Standards

Examples of positive behavior include:

- Being respectful and considerate.
- Providing constructive technical feedback.
- Welcoming new contributors.
- Respecting different perspectives.
- Accepting constructive criticism.
- Taking responsibility for mistakes.

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- Harassment, threats or discrimination.
- Trolling, insults and personal attacks.
- Publishing another person's private information.
- Unwelcome sexual attention or inappropriate content.
- Deliberately disrupting project discussions.

## Maintainer Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying
acceptable behavior and enforcing these standards
fairly and consistently.

Maintainers may remove inappropriate contributions,
comments or issues and restrict participation when necessary.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to:

- GitHub issues and discussions.
- Pull requests and code reviews.
- Project-related communication.
- Public representation of the AutoLoc project.

## Reporting

## Enforcement

Depending on severity, consequences may include:

1. Private correction.
2. Formal warning.
3. Temporary restriction.
4. Permanent restriction.
